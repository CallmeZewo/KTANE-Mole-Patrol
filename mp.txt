Starting sequence of 5 consisting of

 - Position
 - Main color
 - Sub color

Moles can only appear once each in the sequence. Neighboring moles in the List cant be next to each other in the "grid"

First record sequence (ex. in [pos, main, sub] ):

 - T: Blue, Green
 - DL: Red, Yellow
 - D: Cyan, Red
 - R: Red, Red
 - TR:Magenta, Cyan

After watching the sequence at least once you are allowed to press the stone in the middle,
which serves as a confirm/submit button to either confirm you are ready for input or submitting an input.

You will be able to see all moles pop up after pressing the stone. Upon clicking a mole it raises its paw and
lowers it if it's clicked again. If you click a different mole while one has its paw up, they switch positions.
